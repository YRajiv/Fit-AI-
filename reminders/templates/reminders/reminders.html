How It Works

1. Set your breakfast, lunch and dinner timings.

2. Enable the sound reminder.

3. Keep this page open in your browser.

4. When the scheduled time arrives, FitAI speaks your meal reminder.

5. Your meal details are loaded from your generated diet plan.
